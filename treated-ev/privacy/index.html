Treated EV for iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Treated EV has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your car's readings, your trips and your places stay on your iPhone. The app only sends something off your phone when it needs to look something up for you, and it never sells or shares your data.

Treated EV is made by Josh Treat, an individual developer. This page explains what the app uses, where it goes, and why.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your cars, battery and fuel readings, charging and fill-up history, drives, efficiency, test results, saved places and settings.
  • This data lives in the app's storage on your device (and in your own iPhone backups, like any app). It is shared with your own Apple Watch and the app's widgets, and with nobody else.
  • Sign-in tokens for car accounts you connect are kept in the iPhone Keychain.

What the app uses, and why

  • Location, to plan drives, find chargers, gas stations, food and hotels near you or along your route, show the car's speed, and give weather for your range. If you allow it while driving, the app keeps updating your position so the plan to your next stop stays current.
  • Calendar, read on your iPhone to find your next event with a location, so the app can plan the drive and charging to get there. Your calendar is not uploaded or stored anywhere else.
  • Bluetooth, to read your car through an OBD dongle you own. The app only reads from the car; it does not change any car settings. These readings stay on your iPhone.
  • Motion, to tell when you're driving.
  • Notifications, for reminders such as when to leave or when to charge.

You can turn any of these off in iPhone Settings. The app still works, with fewer features.

When something leaves your iPhone

To answer a search or plan a route, the app sends the place or position it needs (not your name or identity) to the service that answers it:

  • Apple Maps and Google Maps / Places: routes, drive times, place search, addresses and fuel prices.
  • U.S. Department of Energy station locator, Open Charge Map and OpenStreetMap: charging station details and prices.
  • Open-Meteo: weather for range estimates.

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Car accounts (optional)

You can connect your car's maker account so the app can read your battery, range and charging without a dongle. You sign in on the maker's or Smartcar's own page; Treated EV never sees your password.

  • Smartcar (for many brands) and Tesla: requests go through a small server the developer runs on Cloudflare, because those services require their app keys to be kept off phones. It passes your car's readings to your app, keeps recent readings for a few minutes to avoid repeat requests, and, if you use charging graphs, keeps charging readings for up to 7 days so the graph can be drawn. It does not keep your name, email or password, and nothing is used for any other purpose.
  • Audi and Volkswagen: the app talks to the maker's servers directly, as the maker's own app does.

You can disconnect a car account in the app at any time, and through Smartcar's or the maker's own account settings.

Feedback

If you send feedback, you choose how it's sent and what goes with it: your note, a screenshot of the screen you were on, the app's recent technical log and, only if you allow it, the day's readings. It is used only to fix and improve the app.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data.
  • No account is needed to use the app.

Children

Treated EV is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
